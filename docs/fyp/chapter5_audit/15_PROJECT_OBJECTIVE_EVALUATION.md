# 5.6.2 Project objective evaluation

Original heading wording is transcribed from Chapter 1 §1.3 (around lines 187–235). Objective labels O1–O6 are report aliases. Achievement assesses the implemented software and available verification. Independent health, adoption or retention outcomes would require evaluation designs outside the available evidence.

| Original objective | Implemented features and evidence | Testing evidence | Achievement | Remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| O1 To Develop an AI-Powered Fitness Centre Application that Provides Personalized Workout Guidance | Fitness profiles/goals, structured programs, camera coaching, contextual fitness assistant and controlled recommendations. M01, M02, M06, M13. | Domain/widget checks, controlled guidance candidate/parse exercises and limited historical Gemini observations. | Substantially achieved | General real-user recommendation quality, entitlement and contextual correctness need independent review. |

Clinical benefit remains unable to establish. |
