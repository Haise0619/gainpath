# 5.1.4.2 — Landmark and mathematical processing

The inference result is a PoseFrame with monotonic capture time, upright image size, source/version, image and world landmark maps, optional whole-pose presence and mirror provenance. PosePoint carries x, y, z, visibility and optional presence. Image x/y are normalized by the upright image width and height. World entries are separate model estimates rotated into upright axes. Analyzer geometry rejects missing, out-of-range or nonfinite joints and generally requires unmirrored input. Source: packages/gainpath_pose/lib/src/pose/pose_frame.dart and apps/mobile/lib/infrastructure/pose/blazepose_decode.dart:170–242.

## Equations and examples

| Operation | Actual implementation and worked example |
|---|---|
| Aspect correction | For normalized coordinates p, multiply horizontal displacement by W/H before using Euclidean geometry: $\Delta=(\Delta x W/H,\Delta y)$. Equivalently use pixel displacement $(\Delta x W,\Delta y H)$. For W=1280, H=720 and normalized displacement (.1,.1), the pixel displacement is (128,72), length 146.86 px. Plain normalized geometry would distort angles. packages/gainpath_pose/lib/src/squat/squat_geometry.dart:55–71; curl/curl_geometry.dart:48–73. |
| Interior angle | At B, let $u=A-B$, $v=C-B$ with aspect-corrected axes or applicable world dimensions. $\theta={180\over\pi}\arccos(\operatorname{clamp}((u\cdot v)/(\|u\|\|v\|),-1,1))$. E.g. $(0,-4)$ and $(3,0)$ yield 90°. A zero-length vector is rejected. Used for squat knee, curl/press elbow and floor elbow/body. Floor extends dot product to world z where eligible. packages/gainpath_pose/lib/src/floor_push_up/floor_push_up_geometry.dart:119–136. |
| Vertical lean | Squat shoulder-minus-hip trunk $t$ compares against upright image up: $\arccos(-t_y/\|t\|)$ in degrees. For $t=(3,-4)$, lean is 36.87°. Curl uses signed atan2 for upper arm and torso, then compares drift from its neutral baseline. packages/gainpath_pose/lib/src/squat/squat_geometry.dart:59–71; curl/curl_geometry.dart:56–73. |
| Calf lift | Heel/ankle displacement from neutral is divided by baseline projected shin length, as are relevant foot motion checks. With 120 px shin and 12 px vertical rise, normalized lift is .10. Toe anchor and ankle agreement constrain apparent heel lift. Recent neutral samples provide median geometry and spread-based noise scales. packages/gainpath_pose/lib/src/calf_raise/calf_calibration_window.dart:25–95 and calf_raise/calf_raise_analyzer.dart:88–105,309–342. |
| Floor scale and alignment | Body length is shoulder→ankle distance in upright pixels divided by min(W,H). At 300 px in 720×1280 it is .417 and clears the .30 gate. Let ankle−shoulder be $v=(dx,dy)$ in height-relative coordinates, $h$ hip−shoulder and $L=\|v\|$. Projection $h\cdot v/L^2$ must lie within .15–.85. Signed hip displacement $(h_y-h_xdy/dx)|dx|/L^2$ is positive image-down. For $v=(1,0),h=(.5,.08)$, projection .50 and displacement .08 imply sag over .045. packages/gainpath_pose/lib/src/floor_push_up/floor_push_up_geometry.dart:184–227. |
| Confidence | Screening commonly uses the minimum of the required joints' visibility/presence and whole-pose presence when available. Default absent per-point presence is 1. Example required values (.82,.81,.69,.90) produce .69 before any additional limit and fail a .70 gate. This number is model confidence, independent of the form score and physical accuracy. See squat/squat_geometry.dart:27–51 and curl/curl_geometry.dart:25–46. |
| Temporal derivative | Squat velocity is $(\theta_i-\theta_{i-1})10^6/(t_i-t_{i-1})$ in degrees/s for microsecond timestamps; a 10° drop in .10 s is −100°/s, which can pass the −15°/s start-velocity rule when the angle qualifies. The derivative is suppressed across recovery. packages/gainpath_pose/lib/src/squat/squat_analyzer.dart:130–145. |

## One Euro filter and projection

The filter holds previous filtered value and derivative. With $\Delta t=\max(t_i-t_{i-1},10^{-6})$, define $\alpha(c)=1/(1+1/(2\pi c\Delta t))$. Raw derivative is $(x_i-\hat x_{i-1})/\Delta t$ and the filtered derivative uses $\alpha(d_c)$. Adaptive cutoff is $c=c_{\min}+\beta|\widehat{\dot x}|$. Output $\hat x_i=\alpha(c)x_i+(1-\alpha(c))\hat x_{i-1}$. On first sample the output equals input and derivative starts at zero. For previous x=.4, new x=.5, $\Delta t=.1$, derivative cutoff 1 Hz, min cutoff 1 Hz and beta .3, filtered derivative is approximately .386, adaptive cutoff 1.116 Hz and output .441. The actual implementation derives raw derivative from the previous filtered position.

- PoseFrameFilter independently maintains x/y/z filter streams for image/world coordinates, resets on stale/changed input, and drops missing tracks from its state. Defaults are 1 Hz min cutoff, .3 beta, 500 ms reset gap and .5 minimum visibility. Squat analysis owns its filter separate from preview; route-specific preview filter overrides are possible. Press and lateral raise filter their angle metrics at 4 Hz/.02 and calf filters lift at 4 Hz/2. Floor default angle filter configuration is 3 Hz/.015. Curl uses accepted raw geometry and temporal guards.
- Projection rescales and centers the preview in the viewport, mirrors front output once, and checks that camera and upright aspect agree within .02. The painter draws only valid/confident joints and associated edges. Display smoothing and display quantization cannot supply evidence to an analyzer.
