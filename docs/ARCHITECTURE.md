`WallPushUpAnalyzer` is a separate fourth strategy using visible shoulder/elbow/wrist/hip/knee geometry and aspect-correct elbow/body angles. Its time-bucketed neutral window requires fresh extended-arm samples and a stable wrist/arm reach, independent of skeleton drawing. A measured cycle requires a sustained bend plus shoulder approach with the wrist anchored, then a sustained return to extension and starting reach. Engineering thresholds are provisional (extension at least 155°, bend at most 105° with approach at least 0.15 torso length held 120 ms, return held 100 ms, cycle at least 700 ms and six attempt frames). Wrist sliding, excessive body fold, scale/side changes, confidence loss, pauses and stale/gapped input invalidate unfinished motion. `WallPushUpWorkoutSession`/`WallPushUpWorkoutRoute` preserve the shared measured camera/guidance/local-store contracts and default to existing Smooth preview. Consent adds an exercise-specific palms-on-wall setup acknowledgement and hides upload. The result uses exact local scoring version `wall-push-up-side-v1`, no fabricated deviation aggregates, and explicitly limits assessment to the visible arm/projected body line; wall contact, force, load and symmetry are not detected. The exact catalog binding is `gainpath/wall-push-up-side-v1`; its active live record was created under explicit owner approval with three tutorial steps and exact readback verification. Instructions are informed by the [NHS wall press-up guide](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/strength-exercises/); the NHS does not validate these pose thresholds.

Calf calibration now exposes `calibrationCue` and `calibrationProgress` to its session overlay. Setup feedback distinguishes low foot confidence, missing side profile, camera distance, knees/heels position and stale tracking. The hold now uses `CalfCalibrationWindow`, a bounded 1.2-second geometry window with a median neutral baseline, at least 80% inliers, bounded spread, at least six fresh samples and 800 ms of stable sample evidence. A weak sample may pause the hold for up to 180 ms; sustained loss, stale input or movement resets/rejects calibration. Readiness requires a current valid sample and cannot be earned by elapsed wall time alone. Uniform 40 ms sample buckets keep the 32-sample bound from truncating the hold on faster input streams. Full/partial motion thresholds remain independent and unchanged. Before any full/partial result, a low-confidence initially selected leg may be replaced by a valid visible leg with a fresh baseline; once an attempt result exists, anatomical side locking remains in force. Absolute projected heel/toe height is no longer treated as proof of floor contact: camera perspective can slope a flat foot in the image. A stable neutral baseline is captured with the member instructed to start heels-down; full cycles still require relative heel/ankle lift and return. The 800 ms hold, fresh/confident geometry and all full/partial cycle conditions still apply. Calf preview defaults to existing Smooth drawing via its own `PosePreviewController` constructor; the user toggle remains, the camera stays live and raw analyzer input does not depend on drawing mode. Squat/curl preview defaults are unchanged. No pose frames are added to stored results or diagnostics.

`CalfRaiseAnalyzer` is the third isolated strategy. Its side-view geometry requires hip/knee/ankle/heel/foot-index landmarks, corrects image aspect ratio and locks the visible anatomical side during calibration. Neutral calibration requires at least 800 ms of fresh stable sample evidence; projected heel rise and ankle lift relative to a stable forefoot must persist before a return can earn a rep.
