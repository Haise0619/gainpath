# Payment preference artwork

The checkout uses these marks to identify member preferences. Billplz determines the options available on its hosted payment page.

- Billplz publishes the original SVGs for Maybank, CIMB, Touch 'n Go eWallet, GrabPay, ShopeePay, Visa and Mastercard on [its payment landing page](https://main.billplz.com/). The ShopeePay source filename is `shopee-v2.svg`. The other source filenames use the local name followed by `-v2.svg`.
- PayNet publishes the PNGs for Public Bank, RHB, Bank Islam, Affin Bank, Alliance Bank, AmBank and BSN in its [FPX participating banks directory](https://paynet.my/personal-solutions/fpx.html).
- Hong Leong Bank uses the neutral bank icon. Its [website terms](https://www.hlb.com.my/en/personal-banking/help-support/disclaimer.html) require written approval to display its logo.
